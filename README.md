# NutriLoop

**Little habits. Better days.**

Gujarati vegetarian meal plans sized to your body, goal and workouts, plus calories, protein and
carbs, water tracking, gentle fitness reminders and weight progress.

- Use it in a browser: https://imshyamalbhatt.github.io/nutriloop/
- Android app: [download NutriLoop.apk](https://github.com/imshyamalbhatt/nutriloop/releases/latest/download/NutriLoop.apk)
  (open the file on your phone and allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser or Files app).

Your data stays on your phone. Sign in with Google to back it up to your own Google Drive.
Estimates for everyday guidance, not medical advice.
